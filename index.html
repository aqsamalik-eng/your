Aqsa Shakeel

Founder & CEO, AEDIEVOL

As the visionary behind AEDIEVOL, Aqsa Shakeel leads the company's strategic growth, digital services, and training programs with a clear mission: bridging the gap between creative minds and the real world. Believing strongly in the natural creativity and talent of women, Aqsa founded AEDIEVOL to help girls with basic education become skilled tech professionals. AEDIEVOL does not stop at training; it actively hires talented women into its own service team—providing flexible, work-from-home job opportunities so they can work on real client projects and build successful digital careers.
